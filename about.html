How It Started

Two Tailors, One Spec Sheet

KILOVAULT started in 2021 in the back of a sample room, comparing fabric swatches that all claimed to be "heavyweight" with wildly different actual weights. We started weighing everything ourselves and writing the number on the tag. That habit became the whole brand.

Today every KILOVAULT piece still carries a charge rating — a plain read of GSM and warmth — because we'd rather you trust the label than trust our copywriting.
